## How I document my work

Each report explains the objective, authorized scope, environment, steps, observations, conclusions, limitations, and lessons learned. Projects include instructions and sample data so another person can reproduce the results.

I label synthetic data and training scenarios clearly. I acknowledge tutorials, hints, and AI assistance where used, and distinguish guided exercises from work I can reproduce independently.

## Publication boundaries

Only my own work, synthetic datasets, redacted evidence, and material permitted by the training provider are published. Passwords, tokens, private traffic captures, personal information, and restricted challenge solutions are excluded.

## Progress updates

The learning log is updated when I actually complete a session. Milestones are marked complete only when their evidence is committed. Dashboard checkmarks do not automatically synchronize with this repository.
